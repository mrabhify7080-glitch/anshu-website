Why Buy VDA Approved Plots in Varanasi?

Varanasi Development Authority (VDA) approval ensures that your residential or commercial plot layout is officially sanctioned under the master plan of Varanasi with guaranteed infrastructure.

  • No Demolition Risk

    VDA approved land layouts are 100% legal for home construction.

  • Instant Bank Loan Approval

    Nationalized & private banks offer up to 80% plot loans on VDA approved properties.

  • Planned Infrastructure

    Wide roads (30ft - 40ft), underground drainage, parks and electricity lines included.
