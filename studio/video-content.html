Service 01 • Video Production

High-Retention Short-Form Videos & Reels

Video is the most powerful medium for brand trust. We plan, script, and edit high-impact short-form videos designed for modern viewer retention and organic reach.

Production Formats

Video Services Built for Engagement

Every format is engineered for specific stages of customer awareness.

Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts

Vertical 9:16 short-form videos (15s to 60s) structured with instant opening hooks, dynamic pacing, and captions.

  • Best for: Daily reach & algorithm discovery
  • Deliverable: Ready-to-post vertical MP4 / script

Talking-Head Authority Videos

Founder and expert-led videos answering buyer concerns, debunking myths, and explaining industry concepts directly to camera.

  • Best for: Authority, trust & high-ticket conversions
  • Deliverable: Teleprompter-ready spoken scripts

Promotional & Product Videos

Highlighting unique product features, service benefits, and limited offers with strong visual storytelling and clear CTAs.

  • Best for: Product launches & campaign promos
  • Deliverable: Script + visual storyboard outline

Founder & Personal-Brand Videos

Positioning executives, consultants and business leaders as visionary thought leaders in their respective markets.

  • Best for: Consultants, doctors, educators, CXOs
  • Deliverable: Narrative storytelling roadmap

Video Editing & Post-Production

Turning raw footage into retention-optimized reels with jump-cuts, animated captions, sound design, and B-roll overlays.

  • Best for: Creators with raw smartphone clips
  • Deliverable: Rendered 1080p MP4 with sound design

Video Shoot Planning

Pre-production documentation specifying shot lists, lighting cues, background staging, and teleprompter prep before shooting starts.

  • Best for: High-efficiency recording days (10+ videos)
  • Deliverable: Complete director's shoot sheet
