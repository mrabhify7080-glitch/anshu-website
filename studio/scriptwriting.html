Service 02 • Words That Convert

High-Retention Scripts & Conversion Copywriting

A high-definition camera cannot fix a boring script. We write words tailored for spoken delivery that hook viewers in 3 seconds and compel action.

The Formula

The 3-Part High Retention Architecture

Every short-form script we write follows a proven retention framework.

0-3s

1. The Pattern Interrupt Hook

Challenges assumptions, poses an urgent question, or states an unexpected insight that stops users from scrolling past your video.

4-45s

2. High-Density Value Delivery

Concise, conversational explanation without fluffy preamble. Paced with B-roll visual cues to keep visual engagement high.

45-60s

3. Direct Conversion CTA

Tells the viewer exactly what to do next: comment a keyword, save the post for later, or click the bio link to inquire.
