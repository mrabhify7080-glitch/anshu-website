Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions About
VDA Approved Land?

Have queries regarding land registry, loan approvals or RERA check? Chat on WhatsApp.

RERA aur VDA approval kaise verify karein?
Aap UP RERA official portal par RERA registration number check kar sakte hain. VDA approved plots ka sanction map aur khatauni records hum client ko site visit par verify karwate hain.
Free site visit kaise book karein?
Aap humare WhatsApp number (+91 83037 27724) par call ya message karke apni preferred date aur location bata kar free site visit book kar sakte hain.
EMI aur Bank Loan process kya hai?
Humara in-house loan desk leading PSU aur private banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI) ke saath milkar 70-80% tak plot & home loan 7 working days me approve karwata hai.
Plot khareedne ke liye kaun se documents chahiye?
Khareedne ke liye PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport photos aur bank details ki zarurat hoti hai. Registry ke time hum saara legal paperwork manage karte hain.
Registry aur Mutation (Dakhil-Kharij) process kaise hota hai?
Sub-Registrar office Varanasi/Allahabad me direct registry hoti hai aur registry ke baad hum dakhil-kharij (mutation) me poori assistance dete hain.
