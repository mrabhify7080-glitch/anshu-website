Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question? Talk to our team — we reply within one working day.

What documents do I need to buy property in India?
You'll typically need PAN card, address proof, passport-size photos, and — for a home loan — income and bank statements. Our legal desk provides a personalised checklist for every purchase.
Do you assist NRI clients with remote purchases?
Yes. We support Power-of-Attorney based transactions, video site visits, and digital documentation for NRI clients across the Gulf, US, UK, and beyond.
How is Anshu Properties different from other agents?
We verify legal title and RERA status before listing any property, and one relationship manager stays with you from enquiry to handover — no handoffs, no confusion.
Can you help me sell my property quickly?
Absolutely. We provide a free market valuation, professional listing photography guidance, and access to our verified buyer network to help you sell at a fair price, faster.
Do you charge brokerage for site visits?
No. Site visits and initial consultations are completely free. Brokerage, where applicable, is discussed transparently before any booking.
