Our Methodology

The 5-Step Content Framework

A clear, collaborative process from initial discovery to final delivery.

Step 01

Discover

We analyze your business, your target customers, your current online footprint, and your competitive positioning.

Step 02

Plan

We establish your content pillars, determine format ratios, draft hooks, and assemble your monthly publishing schedule.

Step 03

Create

We write full conversational scripts, formulate visual direction, and handle video shoots when included in scope.

Step 04

Review

We review drafts together, fine-tune pacing, wording, and calls to action to ensure total alignment with your brand.

Step 05

Deliver

You receive polished ready-to-record scripts, captions, and assets ready for consistent multi-platform distribution.
