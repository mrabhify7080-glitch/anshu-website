VDA Approval 1. VDA Approved Plot Kya Hota Hai Aur Yeh Kyun Zaruri Hai?

VDA (Varanasi Development Authority) approved plot ka matlab hai ki zameen ka layout naksha government ke master plan ke hisaab se officially pass aur sanctioned hai.

  • No Demolition Risk: VDA approved layout par ghar banane me future me koi sarkar dwara demolition ya legal notice ka khatra nahi hota.
  • 30-40 Feet Metalled Roads: Approved plots me layout ke andar wide paka pitch road, proper drainage system, street lights aur transformer line included hoti hai.
  • 80% Bank Loan Facility: Nationalized banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Bank of Baroda) sirf VDA approved zameen par easily plot & home loan sanction karte hain.

RERA Verification 2. UP RERA Registration aur Plot Legal Title Verification Process

Real estate me kisi bhi fraud se bachne ke liye UP RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) verification sabse pehla aur sabse zaroori step hai.

  • UP RERA Online Portal Check: UP RERA official portal par builder ya consultant ka RERA registration number daal kar project details verify karein.
  • Guaranteed Possession & Registry: RERA registered project me builder binding timelines aur committed facilities dene ke liye legally baadhya hota hai.
  • Zero Court Litigation: Verified legal title me purani zameen par koi parivarik vivaad ya bank mortgage nahi hota.
Registry Documents 3. Plot Khareedne Ke Liye Required Documents Ka Complete Checklist

Varanasi ya Allahabad me zameen khareedte waqt in 7 mukhya dastaavezon ki jaanch zaroori hai:

  1. Khatauni / Revenue Record: Tehsil record me seller ke naam zameen darj hona zaroori hai.
  2. Section 143 (Non-Agricultural Clearance): Krishi zameen ko aavasiya (residential) ghoshit karne ka praman patra.
  3. Encumbrance Certificate (13-Year Search Report): Sub-Registrar office se pichle 13 saal ka record check.
  4. Original Sale Deed (Bainama): Purana malikana haqq aur link deeds.
  5. VDA Sanctioned Layout Map: VDA dawra approved naksha.
  6. PAN & Aadhaar Card: Khariddaar aur bechne wale ka identity proof.
  7. Passport Photographs & Stamp Duty: Sub-Registrar office me registry ke waqt.

Home Loans 5. Plot Purchase Par Bank Loan Aur EMI Approval Process

Bank se plot loan lena bilkul aasan hai agar aapki zameen VDA approved aur clear title hai.

  • Loan Amount: Bank zameen ke valuation ka up to 75-80% loan sanction karta hai.
  • Required Applicant Documents: CIBIL Score (750+), Salary Slips / ITR (last 3 years), Bank statement (last 6 months).
  • In-House Loan Assistance: Anshu Property ka in-house loan desk SBI, HDFC, ICICI banks ke saath milkar 7 working days me approval karwata hai.
